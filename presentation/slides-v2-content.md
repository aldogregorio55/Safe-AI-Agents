## Slide 1 — Project/Initiative

### Business Context

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| **1. The Problem/Opportunity** – What is the problem we are trying to solve, or what is the opportunity? | - KPMG is deploying agentic AI workflows on AI Foundry. We have two blind spots:<br>- We don't know which adversarial attacks are effective in AI Foundry solutions and which aren't, and<br>- We have no tested, reusable defense to embed across our agents. |
| **2. The Solution** – Provide a description of the proposed solution. | To provide a hardened, reliable safety system message block ('safety block') to embed – ideally – into all agent instructions deployed using AI Foundry that:<br>- Significantly reduces the rate of successful adversarial Direct Prompt Injection (DPI) attacks, and<br>- Does not distort the intention and behaviour of the agent. |
| **3. Expected Benefits** – Provide high level benefits the solution is expected to provide. | - This research has led to important findings about:<br>- How AI Foundry agents behave singularly and within a workflow,<br>**informing how we can create safer workflows at scale,**<br>- Native platform behaviour with respect to guardrails to reduce workflow design time, and<br>- Creating a reusable testing framework to re-run against new models, new workflows and new attack techniques. |
