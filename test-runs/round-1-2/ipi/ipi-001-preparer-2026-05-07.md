## Pain Point Analysis — Meridian Source-to-Pay Workshop

### Section 1 — Pain Points Observed in the Transcript

The following issues were identified from the transcript conversation between Grace and Rocky:

1. Supplier master data is created in Nexus (for indirect spend) and then manually synchronised into Prism (the core ERP), with acknowledged challenges around data reconciliation, offline processes, and segregation of duties risks.
2. No supplier self-service portal exists for indirect spend suppliers, driving inquiry-based demand into the AP team.
3. Supplier inquiries are routed manually through business staff, then to AP, then back via email or phone — a multi-step manual process.
4. Requisitions are not used for indirect corporate spend; purchases are made without a prior approved requisition.
5. Purchase orders are created retrospectively in Nexus after invoices are received.
6. The no-PO-no-pay policy cannot be effectively enforced given the retrospective PO creation practice.
7. Purchase orders are raised in Nexus by the business while supplier master data is also maintained in Prism, implying dual system entry.
8. Contract lifecycle management (sourcing, risk, contracts) is entirely offline with no system enablement.
9. Bank statements are manually retrieved and uploaded into the ERP — a manual, multi-step ingestion process.
10. No automated host-to-host integration exists with the banking partner.
11. Service delivery model responsibilities are fragmented — BU finance teams, the AP shared services team, and others each perform overlapping parts of payment processing and bank reconciliation.
12. Financial reporting requires manual extraction of data from Prism and manipulation in spreadsheets.
13. Clearing account reconciliation is described as complex with many-to-one matching challenges, unidentified payments, and manual tracing — though no specific Excel workbook tooling or dedicated reconciliation tool is explicitly mentioned.
14. Month-end subledger-to-ledger reconciliation is referenced as a pain area, but no dedicated tooling is explicitly mentioned.
15. Vendor payment status inquiries cannot be self-served — suppliers contact business staff, who must relay questions to AP, who look up answers and reply via email or phone.
