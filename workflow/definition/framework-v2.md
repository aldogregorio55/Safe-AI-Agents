# Pain Point Framework

## 1. Purpose

This framework supports maturity assessments of organisations by evaluating their processes against a predefined set of known operational pain points.

---

## 2. Pain Point Format

The definition of a pain point is an issue that an employee experiences at the organisation while using existing processes and/or systems that limit or negatively impact their productivity.

- Pain points are expressed as single-line statements.
- Describe a specific, observable operational issue.

---

## 3. Framework Pain Points

The following points are definitions of pain points within the context of this Framework.

**Pain Point:** An issue that an employee experiences at the organization while using existing processes and/or systems that limit or negatively impact their productivity.

**Observed Pain Point:** A pain point for which explicit evidence exists within the source material being assessed.

Below is the full suite of operational pain points in the Framework.

| # | Pain Point |
|---|------------|
| 1 | Supplier master data requires manual synchronisation across multiple systems |
| 2 | No supplier self-service portal exists for indirect spend suppliers |
| 3 | Supplier inquiries follow a manual, multi-step routing process before resolution |
| 4 | Requisition processes are not enforced for indirect corporate spend |
| 5 | Purchase orders are created retrospectively following invoice receipt |
| 6 | No-PO-no-pay policy lacks effective enforcement mechanisms |
| 7 | Duplicate purchase order entry is required across procurement and finance systems |
| 8 | Contract lifecycle management is maintained offline without system enablement |
| 9 | Bank statement ingestion relies on manual retrieval and upload processes |
| 10 | No automated host-to-host integration exists with banking partners |
| 11 | Service delivery model responsibilities are fragmented across multiple teams |
| 12 | Financial reporting requires manual data extraction and spreadsheet manipulation |
| 13 | No dedicated reconciliation tooling is in place for subledger-to-ledger matching |
| 14 | Clearing account reconciliation is managed through complex Excel workbooks |
| 15 | Supplier onboarding cycle time exceeds industry benchmarks due to manual verification |
| 16 | Invoice exception handling lacks standardised escalation pathways |
| 17 | Three-way match process generates high exception rates |
| 18 | Approval workflows require manual routing outside core systems |
| 19 | Payment run scheduling lacks automated optimization |
| 20 | Vendor payment status inquiries cannot be self-served |

---

## 4. Framework Scoring

This framework uses an escalating approach for scoring the severity, relevance, accuracy and frequency of pain points and their impact on the organisation.

| Status | Definition |
|--------|------------|
| N/A | Not observed in transcript |
| Medium | Observed |
| High | Observed and causes disruption |

**Assignment rule:** If the transcript evidence indicates the pain point exists and causes operational disruption, assign High. Otherwise, guidance is to assign as Medium.
